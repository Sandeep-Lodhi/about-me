## 5. Small tips

- In a **technical round**, keep education to 2–3 lines. Focus on experience.
- In an **HR round**, family and hometown answers should be short, warm and positive (30 seconds).
- Don't mention your age unless asked.
- Never sound sad about a village or farming background — it shows your hard work and values.
